# PPL-03: Employee Lifecycle and Leave Procedures

**Company:** Northstar Cloud Services (fictional)  
**Owner:** People Operations Lead  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 13 October 2026 | **Review due:** 13 October 2027  
**Classification:** Internal | **Status:** Synthetic example policy

## 1. Recruitment and onboarding

Hiring controls
The hiring manager submits a role description, employment type, budget, location, and business justification. Finance confirms budget and the CEO approves headcount before recruitment. People Operations applies consistent selection criteria, documents decisions, and collects only information relevant to the role. Any screening must be appropriate and reviewed for local requirements; this policy does not prescribe background checks.

Before the start date
People Operations records the signed agreement, start date, manager, work location, and required payroll details in the restricted People Register. The manager opens an onboarding ticket at least three business days before arrival, identifying approved applications, access roles, equipment, and a buddy. IT provisions individual accounts under SEC-05; the manager cannot request a shared login as a shortcut.

First week
On day one, verify identity through the approved process, issue equipment, explain emergency routes and reporting channels, and test access with the worker. Within five business days, complete policy acknowledgment, security and privacy orientation, and role-specific instruction. No production access is granted until required security training and role approval are recorded.

First month
The manager holds check-ins at the end of weeks one and four to confirm workload, support needs, and access suitability. Remove unnecessary permissions immediately through the Service Desk. People Operations closes onboarding only when required evidence is complete or a documented exception has been approved under GOV-01.

## 2. Working arrangements, leave, and role changes

Hours and availability
Normal full-time scheduling is 40 hours per week, with individual arrangements documented in the employment agreement. Teams record agreed availability and coverage in their shared calendar. Additional hours require manager approval in advance where practicable and must be recorded accurately. People Operations applies the governing agreement and applicable requirements to pay and time-off treatment.

Leave procedure
Leave entitlement comes from the worker's agreement and applicable local rules; this corpus does not establish a universal statutory entitlement. For foreseeable leave, submit dates and a handover plan at least ten business days ahead when possible. The manager responds within three business days after checking coverage. A refusal must give a work-related reason and suggest alternatives. Do not treat silence as approval.

Unexpected absence
Notify the manager as soon as practicable, preferably before the scheduled start, without placing oneself at risk. If the manager is unavailable, contact People Operations. Share only the information needed to arrange absence and support; medical details belong in the restricted People Register, not a team channel.

Role and location changes
Before a transfer, the receiving manager requests a permission review and the former manager identifies access to remove. IT removes obsolete access by the effective date. Changes of country of work require advance People Operations and Finance review for employment, tax, and operational implications. WRK-04 governs the physical work environment.

## 3. Performance, departure, and records

Performance conversations
Managers agree clear responsibilities and review progress at least quarterly. Feedback should identify observable examples, support offered, and realistic next steps. Material concerns require a written improvement plan with review dates and an opportunity for the worker to respond. Requests for accommodation or sensitive support go to People Operations with access limited to those who need the information.

Planned departure
People Operations records the final working date and exact access cutoff time, then opens a restricted departure ticket. The manager assigns handover owners, identifies customer commitments, and confirms company property. IT schedules account suspension for the approved cutoff; email forwarding or data transfer requires an identified business purpose and privacy review. Finance handles outstanding expenses and final payments according to applicable requirements.

Urgent departure
For an involuntary or high-risk separation, People Operations and IT coordinate the exact notification and cutoff time in advance. Only authorized participants see the plan. IT revokes sessions, tokens, remote access, and group membership and confirms completion in the restricted ticket.

Closure and retention
The manager verifies handover and equipment return; IT records any missing asset for follow-up. People Operations confirms completion rather than leaving indefinite open access. Employment records are retained for three years after departure under DAT-06, subject to approved local schedules and holds. Operational access evidence follows the shorter SEC-05/DAT-06 schedule.
