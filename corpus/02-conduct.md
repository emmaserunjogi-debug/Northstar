# ETH-02: Code of Conduct and Speak-Up Procedure

**Company:** Northstar Cloud Services (fictional)  
**Owner:** People Operations Lead  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 13 October 2026 | **Review due:** 13 October 2027  
**Classification:** Internal | **Status:** Synthetic example policy

## 1. Expected conduct and conflicts of interest

Respectful work
Workers must treat colleagues, customers, applicants, and suppliers with dignity. Harassment, discrimination, threats, bullying, retaliation, and deliberate exclusion from work opportunities are prohibited. These expectations apply in meetings, chat, travel, customer locations, and company events. Managers must address observed concerns promptly and must not require an affected person to confront the subject before seeking help.

Honest business practices
Keep accurate time, expense, customer, and financial records. Never misrepresent product capabilities, conceal material service problems, or alter evidence. Company assets and confidential information may be used only for authorized work. Reasonable incidental personal use of devices is permitted under SEC-05 if it creates no security, cost, or performance issue.

Conflicts and gifts
Disclose outside employment, family relationships, financial interests, or personal relationships that could influence a business decision before participating in it. People Operations records a mitigation plan, such as recusal or an independent reviewer. Cash and cash-equivalent gifts are prohibited. Other gifts or hospitality above $50 in aggregate from one source in a rolling 12 months require written COO approval before acceptance. No gift may influence a decision, regardless of value.

Supplier example
An employee whose sibling owns a shortlisted supplier must disclose the relationship and leave the evaluation and approval process. FIN-07 still governs competition, diligence, and payment controls.

## 2. Reporting and case handling

How to report
Report a concern to People Operations, an uninvolved manager, or the COO. If the concern involves the CEO, contact the designated board representative using the internal directory. Anonymous reports are accepted through the restricted conduct intake form, although missing contact details can limit follow-up. If there is immediate danger, seek local emergency assistance and follow INC-09 before routine case intake.

Intake procedure
The recipient forwards the concern to the restricted People Operations queue the same business day. The case owner acknowledges a reachable reporter within two business days, assesses immediate protection needs, and records the allegation, dates, available evidence, and any conflict of interest. Records must not go into a broadly visible Service Desk queue.

Investigation
An uninvolved investigator sets a proportionate scope, interviews relevant people separately, preserves original evidence, and allows the subject a fair opportunity to respond. Interim measures may include reporting-line changes or access restrictions; they are not findings of wrongdoing. People Operations targets completion within 20 business days and provides a progress update at least every ten business days if more time is needed.

Confidentiality
Share case information only with people who need it to assess, investigate, decide, or provide support. Do not promise absolute secrecy. Reporting does not prevent a person from seeking outside advice or contacting an appropriate authority.

## 3. Outcomes, review, and manager responsibilities

Decision and communication
The uninvolved decision-maker evaluates the evidence and records findings, reasons, corrective measures, and follow-up dates. Possible outcomes include no substantiated finding, coaching, process changes, training, or discipline subject to applicable requirements. People Operations tells the reporter that review is complete and shares appropriate outcome information while protecting other people's records.

Review request
A reporter or subject may request review within ten business days of receiving the outcome, identifying new evidence, a procedural defect, or a material inconsistency. An uninvolved leader acknowledges within two business days and targets a written decision within ten business days. A CEO-related case goes to the board representative. This internal timetable does not restrict external rights or remedies.

Retaliation prevention
Good-faith reporting and participation are protected even when a concern is not substantiated. Managers must not reduce opportunities, threaten, isolate, or disadvantage someone for participating. People Operations checks for retaliation about 30 and 60 days after closure and records any new concern as a separate case.

Manager checklist and records
Listen without deciding the outcome, document facts rather than assumptions, preserve relevant material, and escalate promptly. Do not run an informal parallel investigation or ask witnesses to align accounts. Conduct case records are retained for three years after closure under DAT-06, unless a hold or approved schedule requires longer. Quarterly reporting uses aggregate trends and avoids identifying individuals unnecessarily.
