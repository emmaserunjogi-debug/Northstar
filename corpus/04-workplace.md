# WRK-04: Remote Work, Workplace Safety, and Travel

**Company:** Northstar Cloud Services (fictional)  
**Owner:** Chief Operating Officer  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 13 October 2026 | **Review due:** 13 October 2027  
**Classification:** Internal | **Status:** Synthetic example policy

## 1. Remote work and shared office rules

Approved arrangements
Workers may use the shared office or an approved remote workspace consistent with their role and agreement. The manager documents the arrangement, expected availability, customer coverage, and equipment needs. A change of country requires PPL-03 review before work begins there. Remote work does not remove security, confidentiality, or attendance obligations.

Workspace controls
Use a company-managed device, lock the screen when away, and keep confidential discussions out of public hearing. Avoid shared household computers and unattended printing. Use an approved secure connection under SEC-05; do not connect company storage devices to untrusted equipment. Store paper records in a locked place and return them for approved destruction when no longer needed.

Office access and visitors
Workers use their assigned access credential and must not lend it or allow unknown people to follow them inside. Hosts register visitors, explain safety arrangements, escort them in nonpublic areas, and ensure departure is recorded. Visitors must not view customer records or use an employee's device. Report a lost credential immediately to IT and the office coordinator.

Equipment and costs
The manager approves necessary equipment through FIN-07. The asset register records custodian, serial number, issue date, and return status. Workers report faults and damage promptly; they must not arrange unauthorized repairs or dispose of equipment themselves.

## 2. Safety and emergency response

Routine prevention
Keep exits and walkways clear, use electrical equipment as intended, and report damaged cables, unstable furniture, leaks, or other hazards. Remote workers should arrange a stable chair, suitable screen height, adequate lighting, and regular movement breaks. The office coordinator maintains first-aid information and checks emergency instructions quarterly.

Immediate danger
Stop the unsafe activity. Move to safety without attempting a rescue that creates further danger. Contact local emergency services when needed and follow the building's evacuation instructions. Do not delay emergency action to open a ticket or obtain manager approval. Once safe, notify the manager or office coordinator so they can account for affected people.

Reporting procedure
Report a work-related injury, near miss, or significant hazard as soon as safe, normally the same day. Record what happened, location, time, affected activity, and immediate action; avoid speculation about blame. Sensitive medical information goes to People Operations. The office coordinator assigns corrective action and checks completion before reopening an unsafe area.

Escalation and learning
Serious injury, threats, evacuation, or loss of a worksite triggers INC-09 and may activate BCP-10. The COO reviews significant events within five business days after stabilization and shares practical lessons without unnecessary personal details. A worker may raise unresolved safety concerns through ETH-02 without retaliation.

## 3. Business travel and practical scenarios

Trip approval
Before booking, document the purpose, destination, dates, estimated total cost, customer commitments, and any specific safety or accessibility needs. Obtain manager approval and the spending approvals in FIN-07. Finance reviews advance payments. International travel requires People Operations review of work authorization and the COO's review of material destination or itinerary concerns.

During travel
Carry only the information and equipment needed for the trip. Keep devices with you where practicable, avoid discussing Restricted information in public, and use approved connectivity. Report a lost device immediately under SEC-05 and INC-09. Do not attempt to recover stolen equipment personally. If conditions become unsafe, move to safety and contact the manager when practicable.

Claims and changes
Keep itemized receipts and explain the business purpose of each claim. Submit expenses within 30 calendar days under FIN-07. A material itinerary or cost change needs renewed approval unless immediate action is necessary for safety; record the reason and seek review the next business day.

Examples and evidence
A noisy public cafe is unsuitable for a confidential customer call; reschedule or use a private room. A power outage that prevents one worker from connecting is a manager coverage issue; a sustained office-wide disruption may invoke BCP-10. Keep travel approvals with expense records and safety reports for three years after closure under DAT-06.
