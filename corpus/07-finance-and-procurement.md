# FIN-07: Procurement, Expenses, and Payment Controls

**Company:** Northstar Cloud Services (fictional)  
**Owner:** Finance Lead  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 13 October 2026 | **Review due:** 13 October 2027  
**Classification:** Internal | **Status:** Synthetic example policy

## 1. Spending authority and purchasing

Approval matrix
All commitments require advance approval based on total expected value, including taxes, setup, renewals committed by the contract, and related purchases. Up to $500 requires manager approval. Above $500 through $5,000 requires manager and Finance approval. Above $5,000 requires manager, Finance, and CEO approval. Self-approval is prohibited; GOV-01 defines substitute approvers. Budget availability is necessary but does not replace approval.

Purchase request
Before ordering or signing, submit the purpose, supplier, total cost, budget code, alternatives considered, delivery needs, and data or system access implications. Do not split a purchase to fall below a threshold. Recurring contracts are assessed on the full committed term; month-to-month services use projected 12-month cost for approval.

Supplier comparison
Purchases above $5,000 require two comparable quotes or a documented sole-source justification approved by Finance and the CEO. Evaluate suitability, total cost, security, support, and exit terms rather than price alone. Disclose relationships under ETH-02 before participating in selection.

Commitment and receipt
Only a recorded authorized signatory may sign a contract. Finance issues the purchase reference after approvals. The requester confirms goods or services were received and records discrepancies before payment. An invoice is not evidence that the purchase was authorized.

## 2. Supplier diligence and invoice payment

Onboarding a supplier
Finance records legal name, business contact, tax or billing details where needed, payment terms, and bank information in the restricted Vendor Register. The IT Lead reviews any software or system access. The Privacy Lead reviews personal-data processing and transfer arrangements. No customer data may be sent before required reviews and contractual protections are complete.

Risk and review
Suppliers supporting production, holding Restricted information, or providing essential recovery services are critical suppliers. The owner reviews them annually for service performance, security evidence, continuity, renewal dates, and exit options. Record an owner and a 60-day renewal reminder for each term contract. Supplier access follows SEC-05 and must end when the service ends.

Payment verification
Finance matches the approved purchase, receipt confirmation, and invoice, then checks amounts, duplicate invoice numbers, and payment terms. A separate authorized person releases the payment. A worker who changes bank details cannot be the sole verifier or payment releaser.

Bank changes and suspected fraud
Verify new or changed payment details through a known contact using independently held contact information, not the number supplied in the change request. Record the verification date and verifier. Pause payment when details conflict or urgency seems suspicious. Suspected fraud goes to the Finance Lead and INC-09; preserve the original request and transaction evidence.

## 3. Expenses, exceptions, and reconciliation

Reimbursable expenses
Reasonable, necessary business costs are reimbursable when approved and supported. Personal purchases, fines, entertainment unrelated to business, and duplicate claims are not reimbursable. Travel follows WRK-04. Obtain approval against the estimated total before incurring costs; the same FIN-07 thresholds apply to reimbursements.

Claim procedure
Submit an expense claim within 30 calendar days with date, vendor, currency, itemized receipt, business purpose, budget code, and prior approval reference. Finance uses a documented conversion source and records the rate. The manager confirms business purpose; Finance checks policy, calculation, and duplication. Claims requiring higher approval must include it before payment. Finance targets payment within ten business days of a complete approved claim.

Missing receipts and urgent costs
For a missing receipt, provide alternate evidence and a signed explanation; Finance decides whether the evidence is adequate and documents the decision. Emergency safety costs may be incurred before approval under WRK-04, then submitted for review the next business day where practicable. Other retrospective requests require a recorded GOV-01 exception and are not automatically reimbursed.

Month-end controls
Finance reconciles bank and card activity monthly, investigates unmatched items, and has an independent reviewer sign off. Track overdue claims, missing approvals, duplicate payments, and supplier concentration. Retain approvals, verification evidence, invoices, claims, and reconciliation records under DAT-06's seven-year financial schedule.
